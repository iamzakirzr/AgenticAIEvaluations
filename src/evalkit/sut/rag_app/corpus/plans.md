# Nimbus Notes plans and pricing

## Plans
Nimbus Notes has three plans. The Free plan costs nothing and includes 3 notebooks and 500 MB of storage. The Pro plan costs 8 USD per user per month and includes unlimited notebooks and 50 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 14 USD per user per month, includes 1 TB of shared storage, and requires a minimum of 3 users.

## Annual billing
Paying annually gives a 20 percent discount on the Pro and Team plans. Annual plans are billed once, on the day the subscription starts.

## Plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Downgrading to Free when you have more than 3 notebooks makes the extra notebooks read-only; nothing is deleted.
