# Support and service levels

## Support channels
Free plan users get community forum support only. Pro plan users get email support with a first response within 24 hours. Team plan users get email and live chat support with a first response within 4 business hours.

## Uptime commitment
Nimbus Notes commits to 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the Team plan. If uptime falls below that, Team customers receive a 10 percent service credit for that month, and a 25 percent credit if it falls below 99.0 percent. The Free and Pro plans have no uptime commitment.

## Scheduled maintenance
Scheduled maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC and is announced at least 72 hours in advance. Scheduled maintenance does not count against the uptime commitment.
