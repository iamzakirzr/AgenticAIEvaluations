# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of the most recent charge. Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After those windows no refund is issued, but the plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear. Nimbus Notes does not refund in cash or store credit.

## Exceptions
Charges caused by a billing error on Nimbus Notes' side are always refunded in full, regardless of the time window. Refunds are not available for accounts closed for violating the acceptable use policy.
