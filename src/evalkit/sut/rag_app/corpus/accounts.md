# Accounts and data export

## Exporting data
Any user can export all of their notes at any time as Markdown or PDF from Settings, then Export. Exports of more than 1 GB are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed within 24 hours.

## Deleting an account
Deleting an account moves its data to a 30 day recovery window. During those 30 days the account can be restored by contacting support. After 30 days the data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Inactive Free accounts
Free accounts with no sign-in for 12 months receive a warning email. If there is still no sign-in 30 days after the warning, the account is deleted.
